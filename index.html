Teacher Idea Lab

The website should make teachers talk to each other, not stare at Annabelle.

Round 1 · Give us your headache

What teacher task steals the most time every week?
What do students repeatedly struggle to understand?
What report, memo or document makes you say “not again”?
What have you tried with AI that actually worked?
Where has AI given you a bad or ridiculous answer?
What would you NEVER trust AI to decide?

Round 2 · Make it Borongan

Which example on this site feels most like your school?
Which example would never work in your classroom? Why?
What local subject, learner need or workflow should we add?
Elementary and high school teachers: how would your versions differ?
Can two teachers combine ideas into one better workflow?
What should the next 60-minute AI Lab produce before everyone goes home?

Round 3 · Teacher Challenge

Pick one use case. Change the prompt. Run it. Find one mistake. Improve it. Share what YOU changed.

The winning insight is not “AI made this.” It is “Here is what the teacher had to add, correct, localize, or reject.”

Human Checkpoint

DepEd’s 2026 AI guidelines put human judgment and teacher verification at the center. The Lab makes that visible in every activity.

AI can be the helper

  • Draft
  • Organize
  • Summarize
  • Suggest
  • Explain
  • Generate practice
  • Help compare approved sources

The human stays responsible

  • Verify facts and sources
  • Protect student and personnel privacy
  • Judge learner appropriateness
  • Check fairness and context
  • Follow DepEd and school policy
  • Make meaningful decisions
  • Approve the final output
Prototype privacy rule: use public, anonymized or fictional examples. No student names, grades, medical or disciplinary information, private personnel records, passwords, or restricted documents.

Where these examples come from

These are not random “AI tricks.” The library is inspired by official DepEd guidance, AI Class ASEAN topics, and published educator examples. A reference supports the type of use case; it does not mean DepEd endorses every exact prompt on this prototype.
